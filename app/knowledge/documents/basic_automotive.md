# Basic Automotive Knowledge

## Vehicle Service

A routine vehicle service may include:

- Engine oil and oil filter inspection or replacement where applicable.
- Inspection of engine coolant.
- Inspection of brake fluid.
- Inspection of transmission fluid where applicable.
- Inspection of tyres and tyre pressures.
- Inspection of brake components.
- Inspection of lights and electrical systems.
- Inspection of belts and hoses where applicable.
- Inspection of air filter and cabin filter.
- Inspection for fluid leaks.
- Inspection of battery condition.
- Resetting the service reminder when required.

The exact service requirements depend on the vehicle manufacturer,
model, year, engine or powertrain and service schedule.

Do not assume a service interval, fluid specification, fluid quantity,
torque specification or replacement procedure without vehicle-specific
information.

## Diagnostics

A diagnostic process should normally begin by collecting:

- Vehicle make.
- Vehicle model.
- Vehicle year.
- Engine or powertrain.
- Odometer reading.
- Customer symptoms.
- Warning lights.
- Fault codes.
- Relevant previous repairs.

A diagnostic trouble code does not automatically identify the failed
component. The code should be used together with the manufacturer's
diagnostic procedure and appropriate testing.

## Fault Codes

Fault codes should be interpreted according to the vehicle manufacturer's
information whenever manufacturer-specific information is available.

A fault code alone should not be treated as confirmation that a specific
component must be replaced.

## Safety

Before performing repair or diagnostic work:

- Follow the manufacturer's repair procedure.
- Use appropriate personal protective equipment.
- Use the correct tools and equipment.
- Secure the vehicle correctly.
- Follow isolation procedures where required.

High-voltage electric and hybrid vehicle systems require appropriate
training, isolation procedures and manufacturer instructions.

Do not perform work on an energized high-voltage system unless the person
performing the work is appropriately trained and the manufacturer's
procedure specifically permits it.
